What Apple says about Duo screenshots

Checked today against Apple's asset best practices, the iPhone Duo "Prepare" page, the screenshot specifications, Design Resources and the App Store Marketing Guidelines. Plainly: Apple does not prescribe one way to show iPhone Duo. Here is what it does say, what it leaves open, and where this design follows or departs.

What Apple recommends

  • Show the app in use. "Use screenshots to depict your app or game in use", lead with the strongest features, in a sequence that tells a story. Captions should be "a short phrase that enhances your visual rather than describes it", kept legible, inside a safe area, focal point centred. Asset best practices
  • Duo sizes and the badge. Outer 1398 × 2034, inner 2007 × 2853 (either orientation), PNG or JPEG, no alpha. Submitting Duo screenshots is what shows the badge. Required from April 2027 for apps on the 27.1 SDK. Screenshot specifications
  • Across orientations. "Share app previews and screenshots that highlight your app across orientations." That is the one Duo-specific creative note Apple makes. iPhone Duo: Prepare
  • Apple does publish iPhone Duo bezels. Design Resources, Product Bezels: Inner Open Portrait, Inner Open Landscape, Outer Closed Portrait and Landscape, Outer Open, in Night Sky and Star White. The display cut-out in each is exactly the screenshot size. The marketing guidelines say to use Apple's bezels "as is": no added shadows, reflections, tilting or cropping. Design Resources; App Store Marketing Guidelines
  • No crease. Apple's inner-display bezel shows the open screen as one clean rectangle. Nothing anywhere asks for the fold to be drawn. Design Resources (bezel art)

What Apple is silent on, and where this design stands

  • Silent: whether screenshots must use a bezel at all (its screenshot templates are for the header and search assets, not screenshots), open versus closed, which pose to lead with, captions versus none, background treatment, and whether a portrait-only app qualifies for the badge.
  • Follows: real UI only, standings first, short captions in the app's own type, everything inside a safe zone, the ASC preview tool as the last check, both Duo displays shown, no fold drawn.
  • Departed, now corrected: round one said no Duo frame art existed. That was wrong. Apple ships Duo bezels, so the default here is now Apple's bezels, as is, for all four sets and the header. They make a Duo shot read as a Duo shot (an inner-portrait screen on its own looks like a slightly wide iPhone). "None" stays available on the toggle.
  • Follows, since 27 October: "across orientations". With landscape allowed on iPhone from 1.7.1, the inner-display set shows the Duo both sideways and upright, in Apple's matching bezels.

Sources, all read 27 October 2026: developer.apple.com/app-store/asset-best-practices/ · developer.apple.com/iphone-duo/prepare/ · App Store Connect Help, Screenshot specifications · developer.apple.com/design/resources/ (Product Bezels, "iPhone Duo", Bezel-iPhone-Duo.dmg) · developer.apple.com/app-store/marketing/guidelines/ (Apple Product Images).

Description unchanged

current text
A simple scorekeeping app for Skyjo that just works. Truly free, with no annoying ads or in-app purchases.

Set up a game with 2 to 8 players, enter each round's scores, and let the app do the rest. Running totals update instantly after every round, the current standings are always visible, and the doubling rule is applied automatically based on who ended the round.

Features:

- Supports 2-8 players
- Live standings updated after every round
- Automatic doubling rule: only triggers when the round-ender has a positive score that isn't the lowest
- Undo the last round if a score was entered wrong
- Game ends automatically when any player reaches 100 points
- Tiebreakers resolved by last-round score
- Simple, fast, and out of the way
- Free forever. No ads. No account. No tracking. No data collected. No internet connection required. Enjoy!

This app is not affiliated with Skyjo or its creator in any way. I just really like the game of Skyjo.
