# Support — Score for Skyjo

Score for Skyjo is a personal project. My mom got me into Skyjo and I built this app so our family could keep score without paper. It has cost real time and real money to bring it to the App Store, and it is given away completely free — no ads, no in-app purchases, no subscriptions, and no plans to change any of that.

Because this is a one-person side project, I can only offer limited support. I am not able to respond to every message, and I cannot commit to a timeline for fixes or updates.

That said, if something in the app is genuinely broken — a crash, incorrect score calculation, or a feature that stopped working — you are welcome to reach out. I read every message even when I cannot reply to all of them.

**Contact:** developer@dtgibson.com

Please include your iOS version and a description of what happened. Screenshots help.

Thank you for playing.
