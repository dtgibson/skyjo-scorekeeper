What you're seeing
♛ Crown icon (new) — Non-color leader indicator in the Scoring View. Identifiable by color-blind sighted users without needing VoiceOver (FR-34).
👁 VoiceOver Labels — Blue overlays show what VoiceOver announces for each element. Rows are grouped into single meaningful units rather than individual fragments.
◑ Increase Contrast — Orange and pink-purple avatar colors are darkened to meet WCAG AA (4.5:1) contrast with white text. The brand indigo deepens slightly.
A Large Text (AX3) — All text scales with Dynamic Type. Row heights expand to fit — nothing clips or truncates.
