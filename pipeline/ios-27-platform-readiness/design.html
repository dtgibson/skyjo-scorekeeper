Skyjo Scorekeeper · iOS 27 readiness

Same app, any window shape.

iOS 27 lets the app be resized — on iPhone Duo, in iPhone Mirroring on a Mac, and in iPad windows. The tall iPhone screen you use today stays exactly as it is; the four surfaces below learn to reflow when the window gets short or wide. Pick a window shape and a player count (the app allows two to eight) and compare today's app with the proposal. The app icon is unchanged.

1 · Scoreboard

Hand-built top bar → system toolbar

End Game and Undo become real toolbar buttons with the Liquid Glass look, and "Round 4" becomes the screen's title. Nothing else on the scoreboard moves. Because the bar is now the system's, it also gets iPhone Duo's side-edge placement and long-language sizing for free.

2 · Score entry sheet

Player rows stay visible, whatever the height

Today the numpad and Confirm button take a fixed 340pt, so in a short window the player list is squeezed to nothing — you can't see whose score you're typing. The proposal keeps the tall-iPhone layout as it is, and reflows only when the sheet is short. The custom drag handle is replaced by the system grabber.

Proposed

Tall and narrow (iPhone)

Unchanged: rows, "who ended the round", numpad, Confirm — stacked. Only the grabber changes.

Wide, or short (iPad, Duo)

On iPad and Duo the sheet becomes a 720×480 panel. Rows on the left; "who ended", numpad and Confirm on the right. Keys grow from 44pt to fill the column.

Too short, and narrow

Still stacked, but tighter: 44pt keys, chips in one scrolling row, helper line hidden. At least two rows stay in view.

How it decides

By the space the sheet actually has — clearly wider than tall, or too short to show two rows stacked. Never by which device it is or which way it's turned.

3 · Win screen

Standings stay on screen, actions stay in reach

The trophy header and the two buttons are pinned above and below the standings. In a short window that leaves no room for the standings themselves. When space is short, the header turns into a single line with the trophy beside the name, and the two actions sit side by side.

4 · Game setup

Checked — one small adjustment

Setup mostly survives a short window: the name list scrolls. The one thing worth changing is the big title, which costs about 95pt before the first name appears. When space is short the title shrinks, the "Who's playing today?" line hides, and the "4 players ready" note moves beside the Start button. Nothing changes on a tall iPhone.
