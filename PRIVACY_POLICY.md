# Privacy Policy — Scorekeeper for Skyjo

**Effective date:** May 11, 2026

## Overview

Scorekeeper for Skyjo is a local score-tracking app for the card game Skyjo. This policy describes what data the app does and does not collect.

## Data Collection

Scorekeeper for Skyjo collects no data of any kind.

- No personal information is collected, stored, or transmitted.
- No analytics or crash-reporting services are used.
- No advertising networks are integrated.
- No account creation or login is required.
- All game data (player names and scores) exists only on your device, in memory, for the duration of a game session. It is not saved to disk, synced to the cloud, or shared anywhere.

## Third Parties

No data is shared with any third party because no data is collected in the first place.

## Children

This app does not collect data from anyone, including children under 13.

## Changes to This Policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted here with a revised effective date.
