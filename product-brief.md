# Product Brief — Skyjo Scorekeeper

## What This Is
A free iOS app for tracking scores in the card game Skyjo. Players enter their names at the start of a session and log scores after each round until the game's winner is determined.

## The Problem
No well-designed Skyjo scorekeeper exists on the App Store. Players currently use paper, generic notes apps, or poorly-made trackers that don't understand the game's rules. The experience of playing Skyjo is great; the experience of keeping score by hand is not.

## Who It's For
Someone sitting around a table playing Skyjo with friends or family, phone within reach. They want to record scores quickly between rounds without fuss, without creating an account, and without the app getting in the way of the game.

## Why It Should Exist
No good version exists, and the gap is real. Because all scoring is local and on-device, there is no server to run and no cost that grows with usage. A free, focused, well-designed app can serve anyone who plays the game without any ongoing overhead.

## What Success Looks Like
A player opens the app, enters names, and moves through a full Skyjo session without confusion or friction. At the end, the app clearly shows who won. It feels purpose-built for this game, not like a generic scorekeeper with Skyjo bolted on.

## Founding Decisions
- iOS only
- Free, no paywall
- No internet connection required
- No user data collected or transmitted
- All state is on-device; nothing persists beyond the current session unless the user expects it to

## Out of Scope
- Android version
- Cloud save or cross-device sync
- User accounts or profiles
- In-app purchases or ads
- Social or multiplayer-over-network features
